Acceptance of terms

Yalla Match is a tool for coordinating sports matches between people. If you do not agree with these terms after they are approved, you can stop using the app and request deletion of an account you created.

Description of Yalla Match

Yalla Match helps you browse matches by sport and area, create a match, join a match, and manage matches you created or joined. Nothing in the app shows that Yalla Match owns or operates sports venues.

Eligibility and accounts

You can browse matches as a guest. Creating a match, editing a profile, and deleting an account require Google sign-in. The app does not show a confirmed age limit for creating an account.

Needs confirmation: Who may create an account, and whether a minimum age applies.

Account responsibilities

You are responsible for activity while you are signed in, and for the name, match details, and contact number you enter. Do not send your Google password or verification codes to support.

Creating and joining matches

The organizer enters the match details, including time, stadium, address, price per player, and contact phone. The price is recorded in Egyptian pounds. The app does not include an in-app payment provider. The in-app help text says some matches show a per-player fee and that collection is through the organizer unless stated otherwise.

Needs confirmation: Any refund or payment-dispute policy. The app does not contain a refund policy.

Organizer responsibilities

The organizer publishes the match details and contact number. The app lets an organizer remove a player from their match or block that player from that match. The app does not verify an organizer's identity and does not guarantee that a venue is available.

Player responsibilities

Review the match details before joining, and choose how many spots to book. The app includes attendance confirmation and a trust score. This page does not set a specific penalty for a no-show, because the server rules for that score are not visible here.

User conduct

The report reasons shown in the app include fake bookings, harassment, no-shows, and inappropriate behavior. Do not use the app to harm players or to publish misleading match information.

Community safety

Meet in public places and use your own judgment. Yalla Match is a coordination tool and does not accompany players at the venue.

User-generated content

Text, photos, and match details you add are sent to the Yalla Match service and may be visible to other users on the screens that show them. You are responsible for having the right to upload that photo or text.

Reporting and moderation

You can report a match or user, or send a problem or suggestion from Help Center. The app says the team will review a report. Removal and suspension criteria are not published in the app.

Needs confirmation: Review time, and when an account or match is closed.

Third-party venues and organizers

The venue, time, price, and phone number come from the organizer. Yalla Match does not own the venue and does not guarantee its condition, its availability, or that the match will take place.

Service availability

Matches may fail to load or publish when the network or the service is unavailable. The app does not promise uninterrupted service.

Account suspension or termination

Account data includes a status, but the app does not publish suspension rules. You can delete your own account from Settings, as described on the Delete Account page.

Needs confirmation: The cases in which Yalla Match suspends an account or a match.

Disclaimer of warranties

The app is offered as a coordination tool between players. It does not guarantee that a suitable match exists, that players have a particular skill, or that a venue is safe.

Limitation of liability

The app does not state a numeric liability cap. Until the owner approves a legal clause, this page does not claim a dollar limit or a waiver beyond what the law allows.

Needs confirmation: The limitation wording approved by the owner and legal counsel.

Changes to these terms

Governing law

The app is aimed at Egyptian governorates and shows prices in Egyptian pounds, but the app code does not name a governing law or a court.
