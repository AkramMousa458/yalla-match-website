Frequently Asked Questions

Answers based on how Yalla Match works in the current app.

Yalla Match is a sports application that helps you discover games, join available matches, and organize your own matches with other players. The sports you see depend on the list returned by the service.

From Home, browse matches and filter them by sport and by governorate, city, or village, then open a match for its details. The app does not ask for device location permission. If a distance is shown, it comes from the match information returned by the service.

Select a suitable match, review its details and available spots, then use the join option on the match details screen. You can choose how many spots to book. Joining is an account action. Guest mode is for browsing.

Sign in with Google and open the Create tab. Enter the match title, sport, stadium, address, governorate, city, village, contact phone, date, time, player counts, required skill level, and price per player, then tap Publish Match. The description is optional. If you are browsing as a guest, the app asks you to sign in before publishing.

Yes. On the sign-in screen, choose Join as Guest to browse matches. Creating a match, editing a profile, and deleting an account require signing in.

On the sign-in screen, tap Google Sign In. The screen uses a Google account. It does not show another sign-in method.

Open Profile, then Edit Profile. You can update your name, photo from the camera or gallery, bio, age, favorite sports, preferred position, and governorate, city, or village. You can also link a mobile number from your profile if you choose to add one.

Yes. The app asks for notification permission and uses Firebase Cloud Messaging for match updates, reminders, and attendance confirmation, depending on your device permission. Settings lets you change dark mode and language. Notification permission itself is managed in your device settings.

Open Profile, then Help Center, to email support, chat on WhatsApp, or send a report inside the app. You can also use the Contact page on this site. Describe the issue, the steps that led to it, and your device. The app version is shown at the bottom of Settings.

When you are signed in, open Profile, then Settings, and under Account tap Delete Account and confirm the dialog. You can also follow the Delete Account page on this site if you need to ask for help by email.
